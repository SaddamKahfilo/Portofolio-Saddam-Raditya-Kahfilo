Student Performance Prediction

Machine Learning Linear Regression Python

Overview: Developed a Machine Learning predictive model to forecast student academic performance and exam scores based on study habits and well-being factors, enabling early academic intervention.

  • Cleaned and preprocessed 322 student records with feature normalization and an 80:20 train-test split.
  • Trained and benchmarked Linear Regression, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting models using 5-Fold Cross Validation.
  • Analyzed behavioral drivers, identifying study hours (51%) and stress levels (19.3%) as the top predictors of student success.
  • Created diagnostic visual dashboards including Scatter Plots (Actual vs. Predicted) and Confusion Matrices for grade classification.
Result: Identified Linear Regression as the optimal model, achieving an R² score of 0.9416 (CV R² = 0.9447), MAE of 2.049, and RMSE of 2.651. Achieved high grade-classification accuracy (up to 95% on Grade E) to effectively identify at-risk students for early counseling.
